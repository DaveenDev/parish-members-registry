The registry as it runs today at Our Lady of Guadalupe Quasi-Parish, Mua-an
and St. John Mary Vianney Parish. Later releases list only what changed
since this one.

**Setting up a new parish:** follow
[deploy/03-new-parish.md](https://github.com/DaveenDev/parish-members-registry/blob/main/deploy/03-new-parish.md).
It runs every migration from 0001 to 0112 and deploys the three Edge
Functions.

## The parish website

- **Home:** today's Masses and the next one, the newest announcements and the next event, the urgent banner, the prayer-request line, Pulong sa Dios (a Bible verse each day with the Catechism's teaching on it, and a link to the day's readings), and this year's sacraments ("Karong 2026", totals only).
- **Pahibalo ug Kalihokan:** announcements (pinned, urgent, by category, with an optional photo or poster), the events calendar with posters, the weekly bulletin with an optional PDF, and blog articles with photo galleries.
- **Ang Simbahan:** the Mass and confession schedule (regular, daily, GKK and special Masses); sacrament guides with steps, documents and fees for Baptism, First Communion, Confirmation, Weddings, OCIA and the Anointing of the Sick, with a checklist to tick; the Church and GKK organization charts; the parish history with photos and a video.
- **Komunidad:** a page for every GKK (chapel, photos, history, Bible-sharing schedule, officers who agreed to be shown), the ministries and organizations with "Interesado ko" sign-ups, the census results, and the prayer wall with "Nag-ampo ko".
- **Mga Serbisyo:** check a registration or request by its reference number; request certificates, the Anointing of the Sick or a blood donor call; send a prayer request.
- **Downloads:** song lyrics for the GKK Kasaulogan and other gatherings (each with its own page, to print, copy or download) and the parish's forms.
- In Bisaya, made for phones, with share buttons for Facebook and Messenger, link previews, a sitemap, and the parish's own color theme.

## Registration and the census

- **Online registration:** a five-step household wizard (household, members, sacraments, engagement, review) with a reference number to keep and a printable confirmation. It lists the parish's barangays and GKKs, and asks every member the census questions.
- **Check Status:** families see whether their registration was verified, or what staff asked them to correct.
- **The parish census:** start one when the parish decides, print forms pre-filled by GKK, record each member's participation and status, compare each GKK with the census before or with last year's paper list, and keep the results once it closes.
- **Census family portal** (`/census`): families update their record from a phone with the code on their form. Staff approve each update, and updates that only answer the census are approved at once.

## Requests

- Certificate requests (linked to the member's record and sacrament verification, with a pick-up choice and the office's own processing time and fee note), joining OCIA, the Anointing of the Sick, prayer requests and blood calls (compatible donors with their rest period, and a call log).
- Every public form goes through rate-limited database functions and returns a reference number.
- Staff are notified as requests come in: a live bell, phone and computer notifications, and a 7:00 AM summary. Phones only see the kind of request and its number, never names.

## The admin panel

- **Registry:** households and members with search, filters, sorting, bulk verify, print and export; a full member record with sacraments, ministries, organizations, service history and History; possible duplicate members and households, with Merge.
- **Dashboard:** registration trends, age, GKK, ministry and sacrament breakdowns, a "Today" panel of what's waiting, and badges on every queue.
- **Reports:** sacramental completion, participation, the Practicing Catholic analysis by census, and a report builder for members, celebrations, households, sacraments, groups, the census, requests and data quality, all printable and exportable as CSV.
- **Organization Structure:** org charts drawn on a canvas and published to the website; GKK leaders fill in their GKK's structure for the office to approve.
- **Parish Website:** every page's content as drafts that staff publish, with photos stored on Cloudflare R2.
- **Settings:** parish name, logo, GKKs (with barangay, chapel, photos, history and private documents), ministries and organizations, staff accounts, notifications, photo storage, the parish email for password resets, and backup status.
- **Safety:** access levels (Full, Read only, GKK leader, Website & requests, Clergy, Demo on the demo site only) enforced by the database; an activity log of who changed what; a Trash that keeps deleted records for 3 months; sign-out after 20 minutes idle.
- Quick search (Ctrl+K), dark mode, phone-friendly layouts, and filters kept in the address bar.

## Running and updating it

- Runs on the free plans of Supabase, Vercel and Cloudflare R2.
- GitHub Actions: keep-alive every 3 days, an encrypted weekly database backup, and tests, lint and a build on every push.
- A demo site with sample data and a shared "Login to Demo Site" account.
- **Release scripts for every parish:**
  - `npm run parish` records each parish's project, connection string and deploy token.
  - `npm run release:db` runs new migrations on each database, demo first. Its `check` and `mark` commands record migrations that were run by hand.
  - `npm run release:functions` deploys the Edge Functions to each project.
  - The steps are in [deploy/04-releases.md](https://github.com/DaveenDev/parish-members-registry/blob/main/deploy/04-releases.md).

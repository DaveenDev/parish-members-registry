The parish no longer has to work out by hand who serves at each Mass. The
office sets the rules once, and the schedule is drawn from the Kaabag list
every month, by itself or at the press of a button. Everything about the
Kaabag schedule is in this one release.

**To update:** run the migrations with `npm run release:db`, in order:
`0113_kaabag_schedule.sql`, `0114_public_census_result.sql`,
`0115_kaabag_directory.sql`, `0116_kaabag_other_days.sql`. No Edge Function
changed, so there's nothing to redeploy: the Kaabag photos go to a folder
`media-upload` already allows.

## The Kaabag page on the website

- **Mga Kaabag** (`/simbahan/kaabag`, also under Ang Simbahan's new submenu): who serves at each Mass from today on. A link appears on Ang Simbahan, under the Mass schedule, once there's a schedule to see, and the Kaabag's page under Mga Grupo links to it too.
- **On a phone:** each month is one compact table (day, Mass, who serves), small enough that the whole month fits on one screen, with the month in its own heading, so a Kaabag can screenshot it for the group chat. Today's or the next day is marked. A small Ipaambit button shares the page.
- **On a big screen:** the next Mass day in big cards ("Sunod nga Domingo", or "Sunod nga Sabado" when the next is a Saturday), then the rest by month.
- **Saturday and weekday Masses** show with their day ("Sabado, 5 Disyembre 2026"; "Sab" in the phone table), and the page says "matag Misa" instead of "matag Misa sa Domingo" once any day other than Sunday is on it.
- **Direktoryo sa Kaabag:** a card with the photo and name of each Kaabag who agreed to be shown, and a count of the others.
- **Estruktura sa Kaabag:** the Kaabag Structure chart, once it's published.
- **Gusto ka mag-alagad?** An "Interesado ko" button for joining, once the Kaabag is listed under Mga Grupo.

## Kaabag Schedule in the admin panel

Parish Website → Kaabag Schedule, for Full access and Website & requests accounts; Read only accounts can look at it.

- **Rules:**
  - How many Kaabag serve at each Mass, a different number for any one Mass, and which Masses have no Kaabag.
  - **Sunday Masses** are always in the schedule.
  - **Saturday and weekday Masses:** some parishes have a Saturday Mass, and some cathedrals a Wednesday Mass, that still need Kaabag. Each day with a published weekly Anticipated or Daily Mass in the Mass Schedule tab shows here with a tick ("Saturday Masses", "Wednesday Masses"). Tick it and that day's Masses are scheduled too, with the same rules for each Mass as on Sunday. None is ticked to start with, so a parish stays on Sundays only until the office ticks a day. A day with no Mass in the schedule isn't offered. A Mass on one date only (a Special Mass, a dated GKK Mass) isn't part of the schedule.
- **The list:** members of the Kaabag ministry come in by themselves from Ministry rosters. With nobody registered yet, the secretary can type in names, and they're drawn the same way. Anyone can be taken out of the draw for a while. Each person can have a photo, for the directory.
- **Generate:** makes any month's schedule at the press of a button. Doing it again redoes the days still to come; days already past stay as they were.
- **By hand:** take someone off a Mass or add someone. A Mass short of people is marked, with how many it still needs.
- **The Kaabag Structure chart** is built in under Parish life → Organization Structure, like the Church Structure, with a starting set of officers to change. It stays off the website until it's published, and shows on the Kaabag page instead of under Ang Simbahan → Organisasyon.

## How the schedule is made

- **Automatic every month:** 7 days before the month's first Mass day, the schedule makes itself. With Sundays only that's the last Sunday of the month before; with Saturdays ticked it can be the Saturday before. It never replaces a schedule the office already made, a missed day is made up each morning up to that first day, and it can be turned off in the rules. It needs `pg_cron`, the same as the 7:00 AM summary; the tab says so if a database doesn't have it, and Generate works either way.
- **A fair draw:** random, but shared out evenly. Nobody serves again until everyone has had a turn that month, and nobody serves twice on one day unless there aren't enough people, and then never at Masses under 90 minutes apart. A Saturday and the Sunday after it are two days. After that it favors whoever has been at that Mass least, and has served least, in the last 3 months.
- **Names stay private until the person agrees** (Data Privacy Act): the website shows someone by name, and their photo, only once the office ticks "Show on the website", which notes the day. Until then the website only counts them, as in "2 ka Kaabag". New people start hidden.
- **The activity log** has each schedule made, changes to the rules (the days ticked by name, as in "Other days with Kaabag: — → Wednesday, Saturday") and to the list, and every tick.

The parish no longer has to work out who serves at each Sunday Mass by
hand. The office sets the rules once, and the schedule is drawn from the
Kaabag list every month.

**To update:** run the migration `0113_kaabag_schedule.sql` with
`npm run release:db`. No Edge Function changed.

## New

- **Mga Kaabag on the website** (`/simbahan/kaabag`): who serves at this Sunday's Masses, or the next Sunday's, then every other Sunday scheduled, month by month. A link appears on Ang Simbahan, under the Mass schedule, once there's a schedule to see, and the Kaabag's page under Mga Grupo links to it too.
- **Kaabag Schedule in the admin panel** (Parish Website → Kaabag Schedule), for Full access and Website & requests accounts; Read only accounts can look at it:
  - **Rules:** how many Kaabag serve at each Mass, a different number for any one Mass, and which Sunday Masses have no Kaabag.
  - **The list:** members of the Kaabag ministry come in by themselves from Ministry rosters. With nobody registered yet, the secretary can type in names, and they're drawn the same way. Anyone can be taken out of the draw for a while.
  - **Generate:** makes any month's schedule at the press of a button. Doing it again redoes the Sundays still to come; Sundays already past stay as they were.
  - **By hand:** take someone off a Mass or add someone. A Mass short of people is marked, with how many it still needs.
- **Automatic every month:** 7 days before the month's first Sunday (always the last Sunday of the month before), the schedule makes itself. It never replaces a schedule the office already made, and it can be turned off in the rules. It needs `pg_cron`, the same as the 7:00 AM summary; the tab says so if a database doesn't have it, and Generate works either way.
- **A fair draw:** random, but shared out evenly. Nobody serves again until everyone has had a turn that month, and nobody serves twice on a Sunday unless there aren't enough people, and then never at Masses under 90 minutes apart. After that it favors whoever has been at that Mass least, and has served least, in the last 3 months.
- **Names stay private until the person agrees** (Data Privacy Act): the website shows someone by name only once the office ticks "Show on the website", which notes the day. Until then the website only counts them, as in "2 ka Kaabag". New people start hidden.
- Making a schedule, changing the rules or the list, and every tick are in the activity log.

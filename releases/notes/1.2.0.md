Two changes to how the parish's website looks: the colours are now two
separate choices, and the home page gets a 3D object behind its welcome,
chosen by the parish.

**To update:** run the migrations with `npm run release:db`, in order:
`0117_hero_symbol.sql`, `0118_hero_position.sql`. No Edge Function changed,
so there's nothing to redeploy. The new theme and accent need no migration.
Before 0117 and 0118 are run, the home page shows the crucifix on the left.

## Theme and accent: two choices for the site's look

The look picker (Parish Config → Appearance, and the palette button at the
bottom of the admin sidebar) now has two lists instead of one:

- **Theme**: the background colours of the page, cards, panels, fields and
  lines, and of the website's sections. **Parchment**, **White**,
  **Champagne** or **Sage**.
- **Accent**: the colour of buttons, links, headings, the admin sidebar
  and the website's header. **Navy**, **Emerald**, **Ocean** or
  **Terracotta**, each shown as a solid colour in the picker.

Any theme goes with any accent. For a Full access account the choice
becomes the parish's look: the website, the registration form, the census
portal, and every device that hasn't picked its own look. For other staff
it changes their own admin panel on that device only.

A look saved before this release still looks the same: it opens as
Parchment with the accent it had (Classic is Parchment with Navy).

On the website:

- **The header and the phone's bottom tab bar** are the accent colour with
  white text, and a gold mark under the page you're on. The parish logo
  sits on a light tile so it shows on any accent.
- **Every section uses the theme's background.** Home's news band is no
  longer dark. The prayer section on Serbisyo, the filter bar on Pahibalo,
  the GKK section on Komunidad, the songs on Downloads and the page
  headings no longer have colours of their own. The hero's gradient, photo
  placeholders, dividers and the registration form and census portal
  backgrounds follow the theme too.
- **Easier to read:** grey text, the small gold headings and Terracotta's
  links are a little darker, so text stays readable on every theme.

## The home page's 3D object

Behind the home page's welcome, a gold object turns slowly in the rays of
Our Lady of Guadalupe, with small stars like those on her mantle and
drifting light. The parish photo stays where it is.

- **Choose the object** under Parish Website → **Home page 3D** (beside
  Visit website). A preview shows each one moving before you save.
  - **Crucifix**: Christ on the cross, with the INRI plate. The default.
  - **Cross**: a plain gold cross.
  - **Chalice & Host**: the cup with the Host above it, in small rays of its own.
  - **Monstrance**: the Blessed Sacrament in its sunburst, a cross on top.
  - **Sacred Heart**: the heart of Jesus, with the crown of thorns, flames and a cross.
  - **Immaculate Heart**: the heart of Mary, with roses, a sword and flames.
  - **Holy Spirit**: the dove, wings spread and gently beating.
  - **Rosary**: five decades of beads, the medal and the crucifix, gently swinging.
  - **Off**: no object. The home page keeps its plain background.
- **Choose where it sits** on computers, in the same dialog: **Left**
  (behind the welcome text, as before), **Center** (the middle of the page)
  or **Right** (beside the text, over the photo). On phones it always sits
  in the middle, behind the text.
- **Who can change it:** Full access and Website & requests accounts. Read
  only accounts can look. The activity log records each change, e.g. "Home
  page 3D object: Crucifix → Rosary".
- **It won't slow the page down:** it loads once the page is ready and
  fades in. It's left out on phones saving data and on phones with very
  little memory. It stays still for visitors who turn off motion, and it
  pauses when it's scrolled out of view.

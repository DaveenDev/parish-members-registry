The registry stays free and keeps every feature. This release adds a few
quiet notes in the staff admin panel about PRO, the optional modules made by
the developer, and a page to read about them. Every note can be hidden.

**To update:** run the migration with `npm run release:db`:
`0124_staff_dismissals.sql`. No Edge Function changed, so there's nothing to
redeploy. Before 0124 is run, hiding a note works on that browser only.

**The notes are off until you switch them on.** Set `VITE_PRO_PROMO` to `on`
in Vercel and redeploy to show them (the demo site first). Without it the
admin panel looks as it did before, and Settings has no PRO modules page.

## PRO tip cards

A small card on a few pages, each about the PRO module that would help on
that page (the certificates queue, Sacraments, the Mass schedule, blood
requests, announcements, the sacrament guides and Reports). The Dashboard
shows one of them.

- **One card at a time**, at most three a day and once per page per session.
  It slides in about 4 seconds after the page opens and goes away by itself
  after 20 seconds, or as soon as a drawer, dialog or the Ctrl+K search opens.
  On a phone it sits at the bottom of the page and covers nothing.
- **Not now** or **×** hides that card for 30 days. **Don't show PRO tips**
  on the PRO modules page turns off every card for 90 days.

## The monthly showcase

Once a month, on the Dashboard, one dialog shows three PRO modules. Any
button, **×**, Esc or a click outside hides it for 30 days.

## The PRO modules page

**Settings → PRO modules** says what each module does, how PRO works, and
opens an email to the developer with your parish's name and the modules you
ticked. Prices are on the developer's page, not in the app.

## Who sees them, and who doesn't

- **Full access and Clergy:** the cards and the showcase.
- **Website & requests:** cards on the Requests pages only.
- **Read only and GKK leaders:** nothing.
- **Never** on the public website, the registration and census pages,
  printed sheets or exports, and nothing is printed from the cards.
- **A new account gets 21 quiet days.** A parish that has PRO sees no
  showcase and no card for a module it already has.
- **Hiding follows the account** to its other devices. The shared Demo login
  remembers it per device. Nothing is sent to anyone about who saw or
  clicked what.

# Census Coordinator checklist: before the training session

For each GKK's **Census Coordinator** (GKK leader). Prepare these before you
come to the census training, so we can set up your GKK during the session
instead of waiting for papers and passwords.

Bring this sheet and tick each box as you go.

---

## Must prepare

These are needed during the training itself. Without them we can't finish
setting up your GKK on the day.

### Your device and account

- [ ] **A smartphone or laptop**, fully charged, with a charger.
- [ ] **Mobile data or load**, in case the venue Wi-Fi is weak.
- [ ] **Access to your email on that device.** Your sign-in details and the
      "Forgot password?" reset link both arrive by email.
- [ ] **Give the parish office your email address** a few days before
      the training, if you haven't yet. They use it to create your Census
      Coordinator account.
- [ ] **The temporary password** from the parish office, if you already
      have it. In the session you'll sign in and choose your own password of
      at least 10 characters, so think of one you'll remember. Don't write it
      on this sheet.
- [ ] **iPhone users:** update to the latest iOS. Notifications only work
      after you add the app to your Home Screen, and we'll do that together.

### Last year's list (your census checklist)

- [ ] **Your GKK's families from last year's paper census.** Bring the
      paper list, or better, have it typed up already.
- [ ] If typed, **one family per line**: `Name, Purok, note`. A spreadsheet
      saved as **CSV** with those three columns can be uploaded in one go.
- [ ] **Mark families you already know have moved away or died**, so they
      can be set aside right away.

### Your own family's details

During the session you'll register your own family on the online form, so
you can guide others through it later.

- [ ] **Every member who lives in your household**, with full names and
      birthdays.
- [ ] **Each member's sacraments** (Baptism, Confirmation, First Communion,
      Matrimony), with dates and parishes if you know them.
- [ ] **How active each member is (A / P / W)** in Misa, Kasaulogan,
      Devotions / Rosary, Meetings / Seminars, Pintakasi and Suporta
      Pinansyal.
- [ ] **Your house address and Family Grouping (FG).**

---

## Good to prepare

You can add these later, but having them ready lets you finish your GKK's
profile during the training.

### Chapel profile (My GKK → Details)

- [ ] **Chapel address** (required).
- [ ] **Puroks / sitios** your GKK covers.
- [ ] **Year the GKK was established.**
- [ ] **Bible-sharing schedule** (day and time).
- [ ] **Usual meeting place.**
- [ ] **A few photos** of the chapel and GKK activities, saved on your
      phone.

### History and documents

- [ ] **A short history of your GKK**: when and how it started, its patron
      and key events. The parish office reviews it before it's published.
- [ ] **Photos or scans of important documents**, such as land titles.
      These stay private and never appear on the website.

### Your officers and the work plan

- [ ] **The names of your GKK officers** and each one's responsibility, so
      you can set their **Responsibility in GKK** once they've registered.
- [ ] **A plan for sharing the follow-up**: which officer takes which Family
      Groupings.
- [ ] **A list of families without a phone or internet**, such as elderly or
      remote households, whom you or an officer will help register.
- [ ] **The dates of your GKK meetings and Bible sharing** during the census
      period, when you'll announce it.

---

## Things to remember

- **Last year's list is private.** Those families haven't given consent
  through the new form yet, so only you and the parish office should see it.
- **Never share your password**, not even with your officers.
- **Come with your questions**: families who might be hard to reach, houses
  with two families, or anyone who may have joined another faith.

Questions before the training? Contact the parish office.
